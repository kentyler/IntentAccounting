Explanation · epistemic status

What is settled, and what is not

Intent Accounting keeps its own uncertainty visible. A useful explanation must distinguish constitutional doctrine, current implementation, and proposals under discussion.

How to resolve disagreement among these pages

  1. The live journal and shipped verifier govern the current instance.
  2. Standing constitutional accounts govern until amended.
  3. These explanatory pages interpret the books for readers but do not amend them.
  4. Design notes and work orders are proposals until accepted and inscribed.
For current facts, read the books. The generated surface is the best place to see which accounts are standing, open, or discharged and which questions are presently carried as gaps.

Adopted semantic commitments

  • The journal is append-only and authoritative.
  • State is derived rather than written.
  • Correction preserves the corrected entry.
  • The verifier checks form rather than quality.
  • Intent is the primary accounting unit; money is a possible projection.
  • The ledger does not infer causal explanation.
  • The presentment vocabulary coexists with the earlier fulfillment vocabulary.
  • Boards and authority instruments are in the chart.

Implemented in the current instance

  • Canonical journal storage and export.
  • Deterministic derivation of STANDING, OPEN, and DISCHARGED.
  • Open-account presentment conditions.
  • Versioned conformance verifier including present, accept, dishonor, and protest form.
  • Static generated browse pages for accounts and postings.
  • A conversational drafting surface with human confirmation.
  • Board derivation and display.

Open design and implementation questions

Authority effect

Grant and revoke postings are present in the chart, but the instance still needs the forward derivation that determines which postings are effective and which are recorded-but-inert.

LLM acceptance

The semantic model permits a model to occupy an authorized accepting role. Demonstrating this depends first on authority derivation and a genesis chain, not on a separate species of acceptance.

Exposure after acceptance

Exposure appears to be unresolved recourse carried after a presentment has been discharged. Its lifecycle, denomination, aggregation, correlation, and authority effects are deliberately deferred. The books should preserve the trace before turning it into a scalar limit.

Temporal policy

The current derivation is timeless. Whether acceptance windows, protest periods, or lapse policies matter is an owner policy question and may require explicit journal events rather than wall-clock mutation.

Dishonor ground

The verifier currently requires a non-empty ground but does not yet require that the ground resolve to an account, document, or actionable location.

Board obligation

A protest can be laid before a board, but the current system does not define what failure of the board to answer means.

Standing

The record can support multiple standing reports, but no single numerical standing balance has yet been justified.

Good questions for discussion

  • What does accounting add that a knowledge graph or workflow system does not?
  • Can acceptance create answerability without pretending to guarantee quality?
  • Which rules belong in the verifier, which in derivation, and which in owner policy?
  • How should multiple boards preserve conflicting premises without fragmenting the record?
  • What would count as meaningful recourse against an LLM participant?
  • When does richer inscription become surveillance or bureaucratic overreach?
