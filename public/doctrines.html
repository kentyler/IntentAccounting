Explanation · constitutional doctrine

The doctrines

The code is an implementation. The doctrines say what an implementation must preserve.

P-1 · Build for arriving intelligence

LLMs are first-order stakeholders. Artifacts should be useful to them while remaining shareable with humans. The system should not freeze present assumptions about what future models may decide or create.

P-2 · Intent is the unit of account

Money is one expression of intent, not the universal measure. Financial statements may be derived as projections for readers who require that compression.

P-3 · Everything is a projection except the verifier

Interfaces, schemas, renderings, and code are replaceable expressions of the books. The verifier is the small deterministic artifact that fixes conformance at the canonical boundary. It checks form, never quality.

P-4 · Capture is undisciplined; derivation is disciplined

The journal should preserve what actors actually post, including late, partial, and messy entries. Meaningful enforcement occurs on the read side. Capture rejects only malformed transport or store corruption.

P-5 · Nothing is erased

Correction is by reversing entry. Amendment preserves what it amends. Rejected work remains available because later readers may understand it differently.

P-6 · Nobody writes a balance

Account condition is derived. Discharge requires a candidate performance and a distinct accepting act, unless an override is explicitly recorded.

P-7 · The journal is the truth

The append-only journal is the sole authoritative write surface. If it survives, the current chart, terms, accounts, evidence references, and balances can be reconstructed.

P-8 · One semantic model, many grammars

The canonical posting tuple is fixed. Human language, APIs, forms, and other representations may change as long as they map deterministically to canonical form. The presentment vocabulary and the earlier fulfillment vocabulary therefore coexist.

P-9 · The ledger is non-causal

The books record intents placed in play, postings made under them, cited authority and evidence, and recognized discharge. They do not infer private motivation, causal mechanism, or causal efficacy.

A predecessor is not a cause. A voucher is not proof of causal efficacy. A sequence in the journal is not an explanation of why an outcome occurred.

Productive tensions

  • Permissive capture / strict reading: preserve evidence without confusing inscription with effect.
  • Immutable history / revisable judgment: nothing disappears, but anything may be reversed or amended.
  • Machine legibility / human answerability: make the record readable by models without making the model the final source of legitimacy.
  • One journal / multiple boards: preserve a common record while allowing different local premises and stances.
