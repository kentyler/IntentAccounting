# Projection

*Draft. Narrowed from a prior version that was withdrawn because it introduced
causal explanation into the accounting machinery. This version stays within the
existing vocabulary: postings, accounts, settlement.*

---

## What projection is

A **projection** is a new commitment to reuse or repeat something in a named
scope, citing the previous occasion as precedent.

It must not mean: "the previous success warrants the belief that the intervention
will cause success elsewhere." The previous posting is a voucher for the
decision, not proof of a transferable cause.

## What the doctrine requires

**PJ-1. Projection is a recorded act.** Every projection enters the books as a
posting. It is not implicit in a rollout, a repeated intervention, or a resource
allocation. The act of claiming that a green travels is itself a commitment the
books can track.

**PJ-2. A projection cites its ground and names its scope.** The posting
references (in vouchers or predecessors) the outcome(s) it draws from. It names
the scope it claims to cover: what occasions, populations, conditions, or
settings the projection asserts the outcome will hold across. A projection
without cited ground is unsupported; a projection without stated scope is
unbounded. Both are well-formed (P-4) but legibly deficient.

**PJ-3. A projection accrues like any other account.** It opens, it may be
fulfilled, it may be verified, it may go red. The existing settlement machinery
applies. No new derivation logic is needed.

## What projection is not

Projection is not prediction. A prediction says "X will happen." A projection
says "what worked here, we commit to applying there."

Projection is not explanation. The posting records the commitment to reuse. It
does not ask why the original outcome occurred, and it does not require a causal
account of transportability. The books track whether the reuse succeeded or
failed (greens and reds accumulate); they do not construct or evaluate theories
of why.

## Relation to existing doctrines

**P-4 (capture is undisciplined).** A projection without cited ground, without
stated scope, or with no subsequent outcomes is well-formed and accepted by the
journal. The deficiency is derived, never a capture-time rejection.

**P-5 (nothing is erased).** A projection that fails remains in the books.

**P-7 (journal is sole write surface).** Whether a projection succeeded is
derived from the journal. There is no stored status flag.

## What was withdrawn

The prior version of this document introduced transport warrants, recognition as
a distinct doctrine, cross-posting requirements for explanatory adequacy, and
temporal-exposure tests. These made causal or quasi-causal explanation part of
the accounting machinery. That conflicts with the design constraint that Intent
Accounting does not construct causal chains.

If the organization wants to reason about *why* something worked, it may do so
in content — but the accounting structure does not require, evaluate, or derive
causal explanations.
